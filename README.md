## 📌 Project Overview

**RoomSync** is a web-based roommate matching platform that connects students and young urban renters based on **behavioral and lifestyle compatibility** (sleep cycles, cleanliness standards, guest rules, noise tolerance) rather than just room photos and price tags.

### 🌟 Core MVP Features
1. **2-Minute Habit & Lifestyle Quiz:** Assess sleep schedules, chore routines, guest frequency, and house rules.
2. **Behavioral Compatibility Match Score:** Algorithmic calculation of a 0–100% match score with aligned habit badges.
3. **Filtered Roommate Directory:** Search candidate profiles and room listings by budget, location, and minimum compatibility threshold.
4. **1-on-1 Direct Chat:** In-app messaging to discuss living boundaries, house rules, and coordinate apartment tours.
